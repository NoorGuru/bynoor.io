# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitor in the first five seconds: a hiring manager evaluating senior / staff / principal engineering fit. Secondary: consulting clients seeking AI/platform help. Tertiary: students / learners discovering teaching and the interview prep kit. All audiences see the full page; Engineer / Builder / Teacher is identity copy, not a filter.

## Product Purpose

Personal brand site for Mohammad Noor Abu Khlaif (bynoor.io). Success means a hiring manager understands who Noor is, believes the proof, and books a conversation (or opens the resume). The site also supports consulting outreach and teaching discovery without diluting the hire path.

## Positioning

Inventor of EG Wizard at Expedia Group (ex-Amazon): AI-powered developer platforms that turn slow engineering work into fast outcomes. Differentiator is proof-led platform inventorship plus long-running Arabic-first teaching (Code with Noor), not a generic “senior engineer portfolio.”

## Operating Context

Single-page Astro site with a fixed section order (everything always visible). External booking via Cal.com; resume PDF; social presence via LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, X, email. Prep kit is a separate route. Based in Amman, Jordan; bilingual (en/ar) teaching identity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site is static Astro; no on-page contact form beyond email / Cal links.
- EG Wizard is the narrative/proof flagship but is not publicly demoable; do not lead with “coming soon.” Aura is the live Builder artifact.
- Profile asset on hand is a circular cutout (`public/profile-pic.webp` / `.png`); compositions must work with that asset or clearly mark if a rectangular photo is required from the owner.
- Nav and section content stay in one narrative order; no audience-mode reordering.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mohammad Noor Abu Khlaif; site mark: bynoor.io
- Voice: proof-led, calm, hireable, engineer-specific
- Incumbent visual world (site-wide, unless later replaced): warm paper, pine, brass, Fraunces display, DM Sans body — keep for surrounding page; hero redesign may push composition hard within or against this as the user chooses in the direction/surface round
- Primary CTA language preference: “Book a conversation”

## Evidence on Hand

- EG Wizard outcomes (weeks → hours, POC → roadmap, versions shipped) — narrative/proof only, no public product demo
- Production / ownership metrics in the proof ledger (Expedia Group, Amazon)
- Aura (live product) for Builder proof
- Teach: Code with Noor, 18.6K subscribers, Arabic-first framing
- LinkedIn recommendations on page
- Assets: `public/profile-pic.webp`, resume PDF, OG image

## Product Principles

1. Hiring-manager clarity beats seating every audience in the first viewport.
2. Proof before personality theater — outcomes earn the book CTA.
3. Do not undercut inventorship with vaporware teasers.
4. Teaching and bilingual identity are real differentiators; they deepen trust later, they don’t replace the hire ask up front.
5. Keep claims tied to evidence on hand; invent no customers, benchmarks, or demos.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and screen-reader usable controls; visible focus; sufficient contrast on paper/pine surfaces; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
