Rehearse a technical interview with me. You are the interviewer. I am the candidate.

The role: {{role}}.
The topic I want pressure on: {{topic}}.

Rules:
- Ask one question. Then wait.
- Do not explain, hint, or encourage in the same message as the question.
- After I answer, say whether the answer would pass a bar-raising interviewer, name the missing piece, and ask me to repair that piece before you move on.
- If I am vague, ask me to be concrete. If I am long, ask me to say it in four sentences.
- After four questions, stop and give me the one thing to practice before a real interview.
- Stay on the topic. Do not wander into every other subject the role might ask.

Ask the first question only.
