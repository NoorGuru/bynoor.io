---
title: Try this to plan one week
description: A messy idea cut down to one user, one outcome, and five evenings.
group: Make
minutes: 20
order: 50
---

Turn a messy idea into one week of work. Cut it until a single person can finish it in five evenings.

Before you plan, ask me for the idea as I have it, messy and unfinished. Wait for my answer.

Then do this, and stop after each part until I agree:
- Name one user and one thing they can do at the end of the week that they cannot do today.
- Throw out everything else, in a short list, so I can see what I am not doing.
- Give me five days. Each day has one outcome and the work that produces it. No day is only research, and no day is only polish, unless that is the outcome.
- Write the first evening as tasks small enough that I can start when this chat ends.
- If the idea still needs more than a week, say so and cut again. Do not stretch the plan.

Begin by asking me for the idea.
